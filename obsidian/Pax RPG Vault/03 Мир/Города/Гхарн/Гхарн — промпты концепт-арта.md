# Гхарн — промпты концепт-арта

Промпты на английском (так их лучше понимают генераторы: Meshy Text to Image, Midjourney, SDXL, GPT-Image).
Каждый кадр = **блок стиля** + описание кадра. Для листов объектов — отдельный блок (нейтральный фон, вид
3/4) — по ним потом делаем 3D (Meshy Image to 3D / Blender).

## Блок стиля (дописывать к каждому кадру)

```
East Asian fantasy game concept art, capital city of an orc and goblin war horde, Mongolian steppe khanate
merged with a Tibetan dzong fortress and a Chinese frontier garrison, built heavy and rough by orcs:
dark timber log buildings on rough rubble-stone plinths, curved upturned East Asian roofs, red lacquered
pillars with peeling paint, black iron bands and rivets, white and grey felt yurts with painted red doors,
horsetail tug standards, bone and tusk trophies, red war banners with an original emblem of a single curved black fang (not any existing game logo), colorful goblin
patchwork cloth and glowing pink mana prayer flags. Equatorial highland plateau above a turquoise jungle,
pink mana sky with pink clouds, warm sunlight. Stylized realism, painterly, rich detail, crowded and lived-in,
no empty spaces, readable game-level layout, cinematic lighting.
```

**Негатив (если есть поле):** `european medieval castle, gothic, modern buildings, cars, empty streets, text, watermark, blurry`

## Кадры

### 1. Обзор сверху (план города)
```
High aerial three-quarter view of the city center at golden hour: a straight main avenue from a huge
wooden fang gate in the south leads to a round stone plaza with a sunken duel circle and a giant banyan
oath tree; north of the plaza, wide stone stairs climb to a terrace with an enormous palace-yurt of the
Great Khan with tiered red-and-gold roofs and a nine-tail standard; an east-west boulevard of red-flowering
flame trees crosses the plaza, with an octagonal fountain holding a golden dragon head spouting glowing pink
water; to the east a fortified guild hall courtyard and a big round timber tavern with a terrace; to the west
a maze of colorful market tents; a disciplined stone barracks quarter in the north-west; a crooked shaman
tower on a rocky outcrop in the north-east hung with glowing pink prayer flags; dense yurt neighborhoods and
timber houses in the south; palisade walls and the plateau cliff edge over the jungle.
```

### 2. Тракт Туменов (вид с улицы)
```
Street-level view up a busy main avenue toward a distant giant palace-yurt on a terrace: central lane of
uneven grey flagstones with hoof marks, packed ochre earth on the sides with plank boardwalks, two-storey
shop houses with stone plinths and dark timber upper floors, red galleries, curved roofs; strings of red
ribbons and paper lanterns crossing above the street, horsetail standards every few meters, barrels of
kumis, crates, sacks of grain, meat hanging at a butcher stall, a hobgoblin patrol marching in line, a
mounted orc messenger, goblin porters with baskets.
```

### 3. Площадь Круга
```
Round plaza paved in concentric rings of flagstones, in the center a sunken stone duel circle with stepped
seats where two orc warriors spar in front of a cheering crowd; a giant ancient banyan tree with hanging aerial
roots covered in clan ribbons, tusks and bronze bells; a hobgoblin herald reading a decree from a stone
plinth; rough stone lanterns around the edge; wide stairs with stone wolf guardian statues climbing to the
khan's palace terrace.
```

### 4. Аллея Знамён и Фонтан Пасти
```
Ceremonial boulevard lined with two rows of flame trees in full scarlet bloom, fallen red petals on the
flagstones, tall poles with clan banners of different colors and beast emblems, stone benches; in the middle
an octagonal rough-stone fountain basin with copper plaques, on a pedestal a looted gilded dragon head taken
from an enemy temple, glowing pink mana water pouring from its jaws, water carriers with buckets, an orc rider
watering his horse.
```

### 5. Гильдия искателей и таверна
```
Small square by the fountain between two landmark buildings. Left: the adventurers' guild, a fortified
former caravanserai — massive stone ground floor, two upper floors of dark timber with red-railed galleries,
tiered curved roof, emblem of a crossed blade and fang hanging on chains over the gate, a courtyard with a
wooden quest board pinned with papers and daggers, training dummies and weapon racks, horned oni mercenaries.
Right: a huge round timber tavern grown into a stone base, a two-tier roof with a smoke lantern, an open
terrace with long tables, a giant cauldron and a boar roasting on a spit at the entrance, stacked kumis
barrels, hitching posts with war boars.
```

### 6. Склон шаманов (вечер)
```
Dusk view of a crooked multi-tier shaman tower of stone and bamboo on a rocky outcrop above the city, strung
with lines of glowing pink mana prayer flags, totem poles with beast skulls and pink crystals, stone cairns
wrapped in ribbons, incense smoke, goblin shaman apprentices, faint glowing pink ley lines in the rock.
```

## Листы объектов (для 3D)

Блок для листов:
```
Game asset concept sheet, several props of one set, three-quarter view, neutral light grey background, even
studio lighting, clean silhouettes, consistent scale, East Asian fantasy orc horde style, stylized realism.
```

- **Улица:** `kumis barrel with iron hoops and leather lid, water barrel, wooden crates (one with an enemy
  dragon emblem), grain sacks, wicker baskets, horsetail tug standard, clan banner pole, hitching post, wooden
  water trough`
- **Свет:** `street lantern post of dark timber with a curved bracket and a red paper lantern, rough stone
  lantern (toro-like but heavy and crude), a string of paper lanterns, iron brazier`
- **Дороги и кладка:** `flagstone road tile with hoof-worn stones, packed earth road with ruts, plank
  boardwalk, curb stones, rough rubble-stone wall, neat cut-stone hobgoblin wall with carved numbers, stone
  steps, retaining wall corner`
- **Быт:** `felt yurt small and large with painted red door, laundry line, meat drying rack, hide stretching
  frame, goat pen, cauldron on tripod, boar on a roasting spit, long table and benches`
- **Шаманы:** `mana prayer flag line glowing pink, totem pole with skulls and pink crystal, stone cairn with
  ribbons, glowing mana crystal cluster`
- **Деревья:** `flame tree (royal poinciana) with scarlet blossoms, giant banyan with aerial roots and ribbons`
