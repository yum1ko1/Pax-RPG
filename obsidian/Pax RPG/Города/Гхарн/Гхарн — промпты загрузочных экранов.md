## Сцены

### 1. Столица на краю плато
```
Wide establishing shot of the horde capital Gharn at golden hour, seen from the steppe: palisade walls and
timber towers on the edge of a stone plateau cliff, beyond them a sea of yurts and tiered red roofs climbing
to an enormous palace-yurt of the Great Khan with red-and-gold roofs and a nine-tail standard; smoke from
forges, red banners in the wind; the cliff drops into a vast turquoise jungle under a pink sky.
```

### 2. Врата Клыка — возвращение тумена
```
A mounted orc tumen riding through a huge wooden gate shaped like a curved fang, bound in black iron and
hung with skulls and tusks; carts of looted Saerit gold, jade statues and dragon scales; hobgoblin guards
in strict lines, goblin children running alongside, red banners and dust lit by low sun.
```

### 3. Площадь Круга и Древо Клятв
```
Round flagstone plaza with a sunken stone duel circle where two orc warriors fight before a roaring crowd
of orcs, goblins and hobgoblins; a giant banyan oath tree with aerial roots covered in clan ribbons, tusks
and bronze bells; stairs with stone wolf statues climb to the Khan's palace; red banners, pink sky.
```

### 4. Великий Хаан Бат-Гхур
```
Heroic low-angle portrait of Bat-Ghur, Great Khan of the horde, a massive scarred orc in lacquered red lamellar
armor with gold plates and a wolf-fur mantle, sitting on a throne of timber and trophies inside a giant
palace-yurt; beside him a disciplined hobgoblin general in black iron armor and a sly smiling goblin
shamaness with glowing pink mana beads; light falling through the smoke hole.
```

### 5. Склон шаманов
```
Crooked goblin shaman tower on a rocky outcrop at dusk, hung with hundreds of glowing pink mana prayer flags
and luminous totems, small goblin shamans chanting around a fire, the pink sky swirling with mana above;
the city's roofs and lanterns below.
```

### 6. Базар Добычи
```
Crowded war-loot market under patchwork goblin awnings: piles of temple gold, jade idols and dragon scales
from the Saerit empire, kobold artisan captives at work, human merchants bargaining with orcs, kumis
barrels, hanging lanterns, warm dusty light, rich but dirty, gold next to mud.
```

### 7. Кузни Счётного двора
```
Hobgoblin forge quarter of neat cut stone and numbered iron doors, orc smiths hammering weapons in showers of
sparks, rows of spears and riveted shields in perfect order, a hobgoblin clerk counting on a large abacus,
smoke rising into the pink sky.
```

### 8. Граница джунглей — война с Саэритом
```
Horde warband descending from the plateau into a steaming turquoise jungle: bugbear scouts in furs and beast
skulls leading the way, orc riders, oni mercenaries with horns and great blades, a ruined Saerit jade temple
ahead among giant trees; above the battlefield the pink sky turns blood red.
```

### 9. Степь Шерракса ночью
```
Nomad horde camp on the high steppe at night: rows of felt yurts glowing from inside, campfires, horses at
hitching posts, horsetail standards against a starry pink-violet sky with drifting mana aurora, the dark
jungle abyss and distant red glow of war on the horizon.
```

## Советы

- Midjourney: в конце `--ar 16:9 --style raw --stylize 250`; SDXL/Flux: 1920×1080 или 1344×768.
- Нужен вертикальный вариант (мобильный) — заменить `16:9 widescreen` на `9:16 vertical`.
- Если генератор пишет текст на знамёнах — добавить `blank banners`.
