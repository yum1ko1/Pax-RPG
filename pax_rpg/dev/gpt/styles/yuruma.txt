East Asian fantasy RPG concept art for the world of Yuruma (year 1047 of the Shattered Sky era, five rival empires).
Hero people: oni. Pink mana sky with pink clouds, turquoise jungles, equatorial highland plateaus, warm sunlight.
Architecture: curved upturned East Asian roofs, red lacquered pillars, dark timber, rough stone plinths, black iron
fittings, paper lanterns, cloth banners and noren curtains. Stylized realism, painterly, rich detail, lived-in,
readable game-level composition, cinematic lighting. No text, no watermark, no logos of existing games.
